### Studio (dark) theme (unreleased, November 2026)

- **Studio (dark)** (Preferences › Graphics › Interface): the Studio look in charcoal greys,
  with light text, a brighter blue accent and a dark viewport that fades darker towards the
  bottom, under a lighter contact shadow. It has Studio's layout (type scale, clustered
  commands, flat section headers, the tree's eyes, dots and hairlines), and it turns on the
  modern look and the timeline strip as Studio does.
- **`oxyde-cli snapshot --style studio-dark`** draws a snapshot in its viewport colours.
